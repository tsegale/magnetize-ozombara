What We Do

Four core capabilities, backed by plant we own and operate ourselves.

Road Construction

Construction and rehabilitation of gravel and surfaced roads, built for Namibia's climate and terrain. We handle shaping, compaction and drainage, and take on maintenance contracts to keep roads serviceable through the wet season.

Earthworks and Grading

Site clearing, bulk earthworks and grading, carried out with our own motor graders and support plant. From cut and fill to building pad and access road preparation, we shape the ground so the next phase of work can start on time.

Plant and Equipment

Heavy equipment and operators mobilised to your site, ready for projects of any scale. Our fleet travels with its own support vehicles and mobile site accommodation, so crews can stay on remote sites for the full duration of a project.

Civil and Concrete Works

General civil construction and concrete work, from foundations through to finished structures. This includes culverts and stormwater structures, slabs and footings, and the smaller civil works that a road or earthworks project depends on.

Road construction, earthworks and civil works across Namibia.
