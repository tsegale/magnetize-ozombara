About Us

Magnetize Ozombara Construction is a registered Namibian close corporation providing road construction, earthworks and civil engineering services. We take on projects that call for reliable plant, a mobile crew and a team that understands the ground it is building on.

Our fleet includes motor graders, bulldozers and support vehicles, along with mobile site accommodation and solar water pumping systems, so our crews can mobilise to remote project sites and stay there for the duration of the work.

From open grassland to rugged desert terrain, our equipment goes where the work is.
