About Us

Magnetize Ozombara Construction is a registered Namibian close corporation providing road construction, earthworks and civil engineering services. We take on projects that call for reliable plant, a mobile crew and a team that understands the ground it is building on.

Our fleet includes motor graders and support vehicles, along with mobile site accommodation, so our crews can mobilise to remote project sites and stay there for the duration of the work.

From open grassland to rugged desert terrain, our equipment goes where the work is.

What We Do

Four core capabilities, backed by plant we own and operate ourselves.

Road Construction

Construction and rehabilitation of gravel and surfaced roads, built for Namibia's climate and terrain.

Earthworks and Grading

Site clearing, bulk earthworks and grading, carried out with our own motor graders and support plant.

Plant and Equipment

Heavy equipment and operators mobilised to your site, ready for projects of any scale.

Civil and Concrete Works

General civil construction and concrete work, from foundations through to finished structures.
